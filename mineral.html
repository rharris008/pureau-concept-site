What's in it

Nothing added. Nothing left in.

Pureau is purified water. No minerals, electrolytes or additives are introduced at any stage of production. The Pureau process removes everything — including the things that most water brands consider acceptable to leave in.

Minerals
Zero added

No minerals or electrolytes are introduced at any stage. What you drink is water — and only water.

Sodium
Not detected

Tap water and many spring waters contain measurable sodium. Pureau's three-stage process removes sodium to below detectable limits.

Fluoride
Not detected

Australian tap water is fluoridated. Pureau is not. Fluoride is completely removed, confirmed in daily laboratory testing.

Chlorine
Not detected

Tap water contains chlorine as a disinfectant. The Pureau process eliminates chlorine and its by-products entirely.

PFAS
Not detected

"Forever chemicals" found in some Australian water catchments are removed by the reverse osmosis membranes at the heart of the Pureau process.

Bacteria
Not detected

The third stage of the Pureau process uses biological filters rated to 0.2 microns — smaller than any known bacteria.

Tested every production run

Pureau sends samples to Australian Laboratory Services (ALS) daily across every production run at our Erskine Park site. Results are reviewed before any product leaves the facility.
