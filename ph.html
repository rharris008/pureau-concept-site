pH level

Pure water. Natural pH.

Reverse osmosis removes dissolved solids that act as buffers in tap water. The result is water with a naturally lower pH — consistent with the chemistry of pure H₂O and not a sign of anything wrong with the water.

Pureau pH
6.0 – 7.0

The pH of Pureau reflects the absence of minerals and additives. Pure water in the absence of dissolved solids naturally trends slightly below 7.

Tap water pH
7.5 – 8.5

Tap water pH is elevated by dissolved minerals, chlorine compounds and, in some states, deliberate pH adjustment for pipe corrosion control.

Testing
ALS certified

Pureau pH is measured as part of the daily Australian Laboratory Services testing protocol across every production run.

Why does pure water have a slightly lower pH?

Without dissolved minerals to act as buffers, pure water absorbs a tiny amount of CO₂ from the air — a process that happens naturally and cannot be avoided. This lowers the pH slightly below 7. This is normal chemistry, not a quality problem, and has no health significance.

The World Health Organisation notes that there is no health-based guideline value for pH in drinking water and that pH outside the 6.5–8.5 range does not pose a direct health risk.
