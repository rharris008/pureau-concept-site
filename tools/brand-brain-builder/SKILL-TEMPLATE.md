# Acme Widgets — Brand Skill

## Who We Are
Business name, the real founding story, what the business does day to day,
who it serves, and why it exists beyond money. Owner's words where they're good.

## Mission
The change being made, for customers or the world. One or two sentences.

## Values
3-5 values, each a heading plus a sentence of what it means in practice.

## Brand Personality
2-4 archetypes. For each: the archetype, why it fits this business, and how it
shows up in writing and in design. End with the blend in one line.

## Tone of Voice
Three words for how we sound.

**Do**
- Specific writing habits. Sentence length, person, structure, rhythm.

**Avoid**
- The habit — e.g. "Never open with a rhetorical question."
  Example of what not to write: "Tired of widgets that let you down?"

Every Avoid carries an example sentence.

## Ideal Client
Who they are. The state they're in right before they find us, in their words
and their frustrations. What they actually want.

## What Makes Us Different
The point of difference that survived the competitor test. Say plainly what
competitors do and what we do instead.

## Core Offerings
What we sell. Name, one line each.

## Visual Identity
Colours with hex codes. Fonts and where each is used. Logo rules —
clear space, backgrounds, what must never happen to it. Photography and
imagery style, including what to avoid.

## Language
**Words and phrases we use:** ...
**Taglines:** ...
**Never say:** ...

## Approved Sample Sentences
3-5 sentences, final and signed off. These are the calibration set — new copy
should sit alongside them without standing out.

## Brand Assets
Links to logo files, style guide, fonts.

## How to Use This Skill
Before writing anything for this business:
1. Read Tone of Voice and Approved Sample Sentences first.
2. Write to the Ideal Client's state, in their language, not ours.
3. Lead with the point of difference, not category claims.

Before handing over a draft, check it against:
- Every Avoid in Tone of Voice.
- The Never Say list.
- The competitor test: could a competitor publish this unchanged? If yes, rewrite.
- Colours, fonts and imagery for anything visual.

If a fact isn't in this file, ask — don't invent it.
