Frequently Asked Questions

About the water

What is Pureau?

Pureau is 100% purified water produced through a three-stage process: filtration, reverse osmosis, and biological filtration. It contains no minerals, fluoride, chlorine, sodium, PFAS or bacteria.

Is Pureau safe for babies and formula?

Yes. Because Pureau contains no sodium, fluoride or other additives, it is suitable for preparing infant formula. Always follow your healthcare provider's advice.

Why does pure water have a lower pH?

Without dissolved minerals to act as buffers, pure water absorbs a small amount of CO₂ from the air which lowers the pH slightly below 7. This is normal and has no health significance.

Does Pureau contain fluoride?

No. Australian tap water is fluoridated by state water authorities. The Pureau reverse osmosis process removes fluoride completely. This is confirmed in daily independent laboratory testing.

Is Pureau tested regularly?

Yes. Pureau sends samples to Australian Laboratory Services daily across every production run. Results are reviewed before product leaves the facility.

Is Pureau different from a home filter?

Yes. A household reverse osmosis filter produces water with variable quality depending on the condition of the filters, the membrane, and the storage tank. Pureau is produced under industrial conditions with daily independent verification.

About packaging

What formats does Pureau come in?

Pureau is available in 600 mL bottles (6-pack), 2 L bottles, 5 L casks and 10 L casks. The cask format uses a bag-in-box design — the inner bag collapses as water is dispensed, keeping the remaining water sealed from air.

Is the packaging recyclable?

The outer carton is fully recyclable via your kerbside bin. The inner bag is accepted at REDcycle soft plastics collection points at major supermarkets. Using a 10 L cask replaces seventeen 600 mL bottles.

How long does an open cask last?

Once tapped, a Pureau cask should be used within 30 days and kept out of direct sunlight. The bag-in-box design keeps air out as water is dispensed, so the water stays fresh throughout.

About Pureau

Where is Pureau made?

Pureau is manufactured at our Erskine Park site in Western Sydney and has been Australian owned and operated since 1987.

Where can I buy Pureau?

Pureau is stocked in Woolworths, Coles, IGA and Dan Murphy's, and available for home delivery through Woolworths Online, Coles Online and Amazon AU.

I can't find Pureau in my local store.

If Pureau is not on the shelf at your local supermarket, ask the store manager to check if it is in the system. You can also order online via Coles, Woolworths or Amazon AU for home delivery.
